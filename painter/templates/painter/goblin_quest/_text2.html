Advantages

Add another d6 to your turn roll if you could conceivably use any of these to your advantage: quirk, dream, heirloom, expertise, defining feature. Pick the best die you rolled.

Death!

When a Goblin dies, replace it with the next Goblin. Kill your current Goblin at any time to add a die to the next roll (yours or someone else's). If all your Goblins die, you'll play as a friendly NPC!

The King Stick

This is given to a player (by mutual agreement) when they do something tremendously funny. They can discard it to re-roll their dice.

Goblinlike Behaviour

Remember: if Goblins agree on something then it's probably true! (Invent the world as you go).

Most importantly: think like a Goblin!
