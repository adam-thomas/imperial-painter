Questing

The quest takes place across 3 stages, with 3 tasks in each stage, each with a Difficulty Rating (DR). Occasional misfortunes (oh no, an Orc!) may increase the DR. Task 1 has a base DR of 4, Task 2 has a base DR of 5, and Task 3 has a base DR of 6.

Taking a turn

Use your Action Token. (Turns reset when everyone has used their token.) Roll 1d6, and describe what happens based on the result:

  1. Death!
  2. Injury, or if you cross off the second injury box, death.
  3. Something bad: the next Goblin gets -1 on die rolls.
  4. Something good: the next Goblin gets +1 on die rolls.
  5. Victory! -1 DR for the scene.
  6. Astonishing Victory! -2 DR for the scene.
