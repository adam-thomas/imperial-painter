1. Action Phase

You may play attack cards and focus cards. Focus cards just do what they say immediately.

Cards have a Stamina cost to play. Each player starts each Action Phase (yours and your opponent's) with 2 Stamina. Leftover Stamina doesn't carry over to future phases.

Whenever you attack your opponent, if they have any defence cards in play (see the Defence Phase), they reveal the leftmost one (from their perspective).

If the revealed card is a block card or a card with a reversal ability, they may play it, paying its Stamina cost. A block card always blocks the attack that revealed it. If your opponent doesn't block the attack, it hits, and deals damage equal to its damage value to them.

After you're done attacking and playing focus cards, discard all the cards you played this phase, and move to the Lesson Phase. Your opponent also discards any remaining face-down defence cards they have in play.

Attack card types

There are three kinds of attack cards:

  • Normal attacks: Whenever you hit your opponent with a normal attack, you gain 1 Stamina and they lose 1 Stamina.
  • Special attacks: Whenever you hit your opponent with a special attack, they lose all their Stamina.
  • Throws: Throws cannot be blocked! However, if the revealed defence card is not a block, the throw is considered avoided, and has no effect.
