Setup
  • Both players start on 50 Health.
  • The player going first starts with four cards in hand, and the player going second starts with five.
  • Shuffle the supply deck and deal seven cards as the starting supply. Place the supply of Sensei's Assistances next to the deck.
1. Action Phase

During your action phase, you may play attack cards and focus cards. Your opponent may play block or reversal cards they have set up.

Each of these cards has a Combo cost on the left hand side. Each player starts each action phase with 2 Combo Points. You must pay the Combo cost for any card in order to play it.

2. Lesson Phase

During your learning phase, you may play any of the cards in your hand to make Wisdom. Ignore their normal costs and effects; instead, each card generates Wisdom equal to its Wisdom value (on the left, in a dark box).

You may learn any card from the supply by paying its Wisdom cost. Set aside any newly learned cards.

After you're done making Wisdom and learning things, discard your played cards and go to the end phase.
