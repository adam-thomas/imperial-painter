2. Learning Phase

During your learning phase, you may play any of the cards in your hand to make Wisdom. Ignore their normal costs and effects; instead, each card generates Wisdom equal to its Wisdom value (on the left, in a dark box).

You may learn any card from the supply by paying its Wisdom cost. Set aside any newly learned cards.

After you're done making Wisdom and learning things, discard your played cards and go to the end phase.

3. End Phase
  • Discard any cards left in your hand.
  • For each new card you learned this turn, trash a card from your discard pile and add the new card in its place.
  • If the supply has fewer than seven cards in it, add new ones from the supply deck until it's back to seven.
  • Finally, draw a new hand of five cards.
Other general rules

If at any point you try to draw from an empty deck, shuffle your discard pile and turn it over to form a new deck, then draw.

Some cards have a Reversal ability. These abilities can be used when they meet a certain condition, usually something your opponent does. This might allow you to play the card or to discard it for an effect.
