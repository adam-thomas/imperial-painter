2. Lesson Phase

During your Lesson Phase, you may play any of the cards in your hand to make Wisdom. Ignore their normal costs and effects; instead, each card generates Wisdom equal to its Wisdom value (top left).

You may learn any card from the supply by paying its cost in Wisdom (bottom right). Set aside any newly learned cards. Then discard all cards that you played for Wisdom this phase.

3. Defence Phase

You may play any cards from your hand face-down in front of you as defence cards, and arrange your defence cards in any order. Once you end this phase, the order is locked in, so be careful to place them correctly!

Whenever your opponent attacks you, if you have any defence cards in play, you reveal the leftmost one. If it's a block or reversal, you have the option to play it on the attack that revealed it, paying its Stamina cost as normal. (Remember that getting hit makes you lose Stamina - one for a normal, all of it for a special or throw.)

4. End Phase
  • Discard any cards left in your hand.
  • For each new card you learned this turn, trash a card from your discard pile and add the new card in its place.
  • If the supply has fewer than seven cards in it, add new ones from the supply deck until it's back to seven.
  • Finally, draw a new hand of five cards.
