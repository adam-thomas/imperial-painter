2. Lesson Phase

During your Lesson Phase, you may play any of the cards in your hand to make Wisdom. Ignore their normal costs and effects; instead, each card generates Wisdom equal to its Wisdom value (on the left, in a dark box).

You may learn any card from the supply by paying its Wisdom cost. Set aside any newly learned cards. Then discard all cards that you played for Wisdom this phase.

3. Defence Phase

You may play any cards from your hand face-down in front of you as defence cards.

These are revealed when your opponent attacks you in their next turn, one for each attack. When a defence card is revealed, if it's a block or reversal, you have the option to play it on the attack that revealed it. Defence cards are revealed in strict order - left to right - so be careful to place them correctly!

4. End Phase
  • Discard any cards left in your hand.
  • For each new card you learned this turn, trash a card from your discard pile and add the new card in its place.
  • If the supply has fewer than seven cards in it, add new ones from the supply deck until it's back to seven.
  • Finally, draw a new hand of five cards.
